Investor Questions

❓ 1. What problem are you solving?

Today, launching a production-ready website and mobile app takes months, a full engineering team, and significant upfront cost. Most founders either burn money or never launch at all.

❓ 2. What exactly does Keynate do?

Keynate provides ready-made, category-based software products. A founder selects a category, applies branding and configuration, and goes live on their own server in under 15 minutes.

❓ 3. Is this a no-code tool or website builder?

No. This is not a builder or a no-code tool. Keynate delivers full production systems — the same type of software a company would normally build with an engineering team. These systems are already live and used by paying customers.

❓ 4. Who is your customer?

Our customers are founders, media companies, and businesses that want to launch fast but don’t want to hire or manage engineering teams.

Today we’re focused on content platforms and e-commerce.

❓ 5. How do you make money?

We charge customers for setup and ongoing usage. Pricing scales based on product type and features.

It’s a combination of one-time deployment and recurring revenue.

❓ 6. Why can’t someone else do this?

❓ 7. Why will you win?

Speed and ownership. We reduce time-to-market from months to minutes, and customers fully own their infrastructure — no lock-in.

❓ 8. What traction do you have?

We’re revenue-generating and growing organically.
