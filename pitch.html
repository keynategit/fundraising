Keynate

Imagine if starting an online business didn’t begin with hiring developers, choosing tech stacks, or waiting months to go live.

Imagine if you could start with a real, fully operational product on day one.

That’s the problem Keynate solves.

Keynate removes the build phase from launching digital businesses. We provide category-specific, production-ready business platforms that founders and companies can launch instantly — connect a domain, configure the brand, and start operating instead of building.

These are not templates, and we’re not selling code. Every launch is a complete, running system — including website, mobile apps, backend, admin, hosting, SEO, and monetization — ready for customers and revenue from day one.

We started with news and content platforms because speed to market and operational reliability matter most there. This model is already validated, with 40+ paying customers running live production businesses on Keynate today.

Our advantage is repeatability. One standardized core platform powers every launch, allowing us to deploy businesses again and again without rebuilding software.

As we expand into categories like e-commerce, restaurants, hotels, and creator platforms, Keynate becomes the infrastructure layer that turns launching an online business from a months-long project into a minutes-long process.

Investor Questions

❓ 1. What problem are you solving?

Today, launching a real online business requires hiring engineers, choosing tech stacks, managing infrastructure, and waiting months to go live. This high cost and long build cycle prevents founders from validating ideas and causes many businesses to fail before launch.

❓ 2. What exactly does Keynate do?

Keynate provides category-specific, production-ready business platforms. Founders connect a domain, configure their brand, and go live instantly with a complete system including website, mobile apps, backend, admin dashboard, hosting, SEO, and automation.

❓ 3. Is this a no-code tool or website builder?

No. Keynate is not a website builder or no-code tool. Each launch is a full production system — the same software a company would normally spend months building with an engineering team.

❓ 4. Who is your customer?

Our customers are founders, publishers, and businesses that want to launch digital platforms quickly without hiring or managing engineering teams.

Our initial vertical is news and content platforms, with expansion planned into e-commerce, creators, education, hospitality, and other business categories.

❓ 5. How do you make money?

Keynate follows a SaaS model. Customers pay recurring subscriptions for platform access, managed hosting, updates, monitoring, and automation.

Early customers paid upfront deployment fees, which validated strong demand. We are now transitioning fully to predictable recurring revenue.

❓ 6. Why can’t someone else do this?

While the idea appears simple, building standardized software that launches instantly, works across categories, and runs reliably in production requires deep system architecture and years of engineering. This infrastructure already exists and is actively used by customers today.

❓ 7. Why will you win?

Our advantage is repeatability. One standardized core platform powers every deployment, allowing us to launch businesses repeatedly without rebuilding software. This creates speed, reliability, and strong operating leverage.

❓ 8. What traction do you have?

Keynate is live with 40+ paying customers running production businesses on real domains. The platform generates recurring revenue and validates strong demand for instant business-launch infrastructure.

❓ 9. How big can this be?

Every online business requires software. By standardizing launch infrastructure across multiple industries, Keynate becomes the operating layer for digital businesses globally — similar to how Shopify standardized commerce.

❓ 10. Why you?

The entire platform was built end-to-end by the founder, from backend architecture to infrastructure and automation. This deep technical ownership enables rapid execution, tight feedback loops, and long-term platform scalability.

❓ 11. What are the risks?

The primary risk is scaling distribution and customer acquisition faster than engineering. Capital enables team expansion, automation, and structured go-to-market execution.

❓ 12. What are you raising and why?

We are raising capital to accelerate automation, hire a small core engineering team, expand cloud infrastructure, and scale go-to-market.

This allows us to grow recurring revenue and establish Keynate as the default platform for launching online businesses.
